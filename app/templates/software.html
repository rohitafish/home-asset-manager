Red: a high or critical vulnerability, a pending security update, end of life, disabled by Homebrew, or the installed version published more than 5 years ago. Amber: other known vulnerabilities, any other pending update, deprecated or no-longer-packaged software, Intel-only Mac apps, end of life within 6 months, or published more than 2 years ago. Linux distribution packages are only marked amber for age, and only past 5 years, because the distribution backports security fixes into older versions. Chrome, Firefox and Edge get no early end-of-life warning, since a new version ships every few weeks; they turn red once a newer version is out and the machine hasn't taken it. Unrated: nothing to judge by. A green row means no known issues among what was checked, not that the software was scanned for everything.
